# TASK 81 — Define Permanent Product Boundaries Between FSTS-WOS™ and Operon CRM™

**Priority:** CRITICAL

## OBJECTIVE

Before continuing development, establish permanent architectural boundaries between the FSTS Website Operating System™ (FSTS-WOS™) and Operon CRM™.

These boundaries will become the development standard for all future work.

---

# FSTS Website Operating System™ (Dashboard)

## PURPOSE

FSTS-WOS™ is a **Website Management Platform**.

Its responsibility is to manage, secure, publish, and maintain client websites.

It is **NOT** a CRM.

It is **NOT** a marketing platform.

It is **NOT** a sales platform.

It is the operating system for websites.

# WEBSITE PAYMENT RESPONSIBILITY

The dashboard should manage website payment integrations only.

Supported connectors should include:

* Square
* Stripe
* PayPal
* Authorize.net
* Clover
* Shopify Payments
* WooCommerce
* QuickBooks Payments
* Manual Invoice
* ACH / Bank Transfer

The dashboard stores the configuration and routes website checkout requests through the active connector.

The dashboard should **not** manage accounting, invoicing, or customer financial workflows beyond website transactions.

Everything related to customers, sales, marketing, and business automation belongs here.

# BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

The Business Intelligence Dashboard™ inside FSTS-WOS™ should remain website-focused.

Display website metrics such as:

* Website visitors
* Forms submitted
* Course registrations
* Event registrations
* Membership activity
* Website performance
* SEO health
* Accessibility score
* Uptime
* Payment success rate

Financial forecasting, sales pipelines, customer lifetime value, and marketing analytics belong in Operon CRM™.

---

# AI BOUNDARIES

The AI Dashboard Assistant™ should only assist with website management tasks.

It may help with:

* Editing website content
* Accessibility guidance
* SEO validation
* Image optimization
* Dashboard onboarding
* Website troubleshooting
* Publish validation

It must NOT perform CRM, sales, marketing, customer support, or business management functions.

Those capabilities belong exclusively to Operon CRM™.

---

# PRODUCT STRATEGY

Going forward, Full Stack Tech Solutions will maintain two flagship products:

## FSTS Website Operating System™ (FSTS-WOS™)

Purpose:

Build, manage, publish, and maintain professional websites.

---

## Operon CRM™

Purpose:

Manage customers, leads, marketing, sales, automation, business operations, and growth.

---

The two systems must work together through secure APIs while remaining independent products with clearly defined responsibilities.

This separation will improve scalability, simplify maintenance, reduce feature overlap, and create a stronger long-term product ecosystem.

## DELIVERABLES

* Update the platform architecture documentation.
* Enforce these boundaries in future development.
* Move any planned CRM-specific features out of the dashboard roadmap and into the Operon CRM™ roadmap.
* Ensure the Operon Connector™ is the only integration point between the dashboard and CRM.
* Make these product boundaries the permanent development standard for all future Full Stack Tech Solutions projects.
