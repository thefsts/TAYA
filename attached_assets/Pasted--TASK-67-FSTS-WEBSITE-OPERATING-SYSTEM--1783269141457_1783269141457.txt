PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Corsair Tactical Solutions is now the first live production website powered by the FSTS Website Operating System™ (FSTS-WOS).

From this point forward, we are transitioning from building individual websites to building a scalable software platform.

The goal of this phase is to stabilize, standardize, document, and prepare the platform for onboarding all future client websites.

DO NOT redesign the dashboard.

DO NOT redesign the websites.

Focus on platform quality, scalability, maintainability, documentation, and production readiness.
