## Section Title

**The 4D Protection Model™**

### Introduction

The **4D Protection Model™** is CTS's proprietary security framework built around four sequential phases designed to proactively protect people, property, and organizations while reducing risk through progressive response strategies.

---

## Deter

**The First Line of Defense**

Visible security presence, signage, lighting, and access control measures discourage threats before they materialize.

---

## Detect

**Identify Threats Early**

Surveillance systems, trained officer observation, and situational awareness protocols identify potential threats early, creating valuable time and space to respond appropriately.

---

## Deflect

**The CTS Difference**

This is the distinctive Corsair Tactical Solutions differentiator.

Officers are trained in verbal de-escalation, conflict management, and trauma-informed response techniques that redirect threats without unnecessary force.

Most security models move directly to physical intervention.

CTS treats communication, professionalism, and conflict resolution as a critical middle layer that protects clients while reducing unnecessary escalation.

---

## Defend

**Professional Response When Necessary**

The final escalation tier activates only when the other three phases have been exhausted or bypassed.

This includes:

* Physical intervention
* Emergency response
* Life safety protection
* Coordination with law enforcement

---

## Progressive Escalation

The model is structured as a progressive escalation ladder, meaning each phase only activates when the previous one is insufficient.

The objective is to resolve situations as early as possible, minimizing risk to personnel, clients, visitors, and bystanders.

The **Deflect** layer reflects CTS's philosophy:

**A well-trained officer is a communicator first and a physical responder only when necessary.**
