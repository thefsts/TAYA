43. Responsive Design & Mobile Optimization Verification

Priority: CRITICAL

As part of these image updates, perform a complete responsive design review to ensure the website delivers an excellent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

This review should include every page, every image, every form, and every interactive element.

## A. Responsive Image Verification

Review every image on the website, including the newly updated course images.

Verify:

* Images display correctly on desktop.
* Images display correctly on tablets.
* Images display correctly on mobile phones.
* No faces or important subjects are cropped.
* Images maintain consistent aspect ratios.
* No stretching or distortion.
* Responsive image sizes are served where appropriate.
* Lazy loading is enabled where appropriate.
* Images remain optimized for fast loading.

---

## B. Mobile Layout Review

Test the website on common mobile screen sizes, including:

* iPhone SE
* iPhone 14/15/16
* iPhone Pro Max
* Samsung Galaxy devices
* Google Pixel devices

Verify:

* No horizontal scrolling.
* No overlapping content.
* No cut-off text.
* Proper spacing throughout.
* Buttons are easy to tap (minimum 44×44 px).
* Navigation menu functions correctly.
* Hero sections display properly.
* Cards stack correctly.
* Images resize appropriately.
* Forms remain easy to complete.

---

## C. Tablet Review

Test common tablet breakpoints, including:

* iPad Mini
* iPad
* iPad Pro
* Android tablets

Verify:

* Proper spacing.
* Responsive grids.
* Correct image scaling.
* Readable typography.
* Navigation behaves correctly.
* No layout shifts.

---

## D. Typography Review

Verify typography across the site.

Standards:

* Body text: minimum 16px on mobile.
* Supporting text: minimum 14px.
* Headings scale proportionally.
* Comfortable line spacing.
* Good color contrast.
* No text clipping or overflow.

---

## E. Forms & Checkout Review

Verify on mobile and tablet:

* Contact forms.

* Square checkout.
* Waiver forms.
* Quick Consult forms.

Confirm:

* Fields remain easy to use.

* Buttons remain accessible.
