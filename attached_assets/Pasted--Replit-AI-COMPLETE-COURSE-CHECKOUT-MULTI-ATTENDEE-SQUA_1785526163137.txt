# Primary Problems to Fix

The current system has several reported problems:

1. Course emails are not being sent reliably.
2. When Square receives the order, the customer may appear as `Private` instead of the actual purchaser’s name.
3. Resend webhooks are not visible or may not exist.
4. Square webhook setup may be incomplete.
5. Customers need to add additional attendees to one course order.
6. Orders with five or more attendees must be identified as group registrations.
7. Course checkout needs a promo-code field.
8. The approved promo codes already exist in Square but may not automatically work with the current custom checkout.
9. The website, Square order, registration record, confirmation page, and emails must all show the same totals.
10. Production must not be switched publicly to Square Sandbox.

# Critical Production Rule

Do not automatically move the public website from Square Production to Sandbox.

Square Sandbox may be used for testing.

The production site must never accept Sandbox payments from customers.

Classify the production status after the audit:

## GREEN

Keep Production active when:

- Payments complete correctly.
- Amounts are correct.
- Customer names are attached.
- Orders are itemized.
- Registrations are stored.
- Square webhooks work.
- Resend emails work.

## YELLOW

Keep Production active but temporarily disable only affected course checkout when:

- Payments are correct.
- The main problem is email or registration synchronization.
- Existing paid registrations can be recovered safely.

## RED

Temporarily disable all public checkout when:

- Wrong amounts are charged.
- Duplicate payments occur.
- Payments use mixed Sandbox and Production credentials.
- Orders cannot be reconciled.
- Customers are being charged without usable registration records.
- Square customer/payment status cannot be trusted.

If checkout must be disabled, show:

```text
Online registration is temporarily unavailable while we complete a payment-system update. Please contact Corsair Tactical Solutions for assistance.
```

Do not silently redirect customers into Sandbox.
