# BODY CONTENT

## Protecting Houses of Worship

Corsair Tactical Solutions partners with church leadership teams to develop, strengthen, and implement comprehensive security plans for houses of worship.

Through professional security assessments, specialized training, and onsite armed or unarmed security services, we help create a safer environment for congregations, staff, volunteers, and visitors.

---

## Protecting Those We Will Never Meet.

We begin with non-lethal de-escalation training, volunteer safety protocols, and medical emergency readiness.

Our initial consultation includes:

- A comprehensive walk-through of your facility
- A detailed meeting with church leadership
- Evaluation of current security procedures
- Review of emergency planning
- A written scope document outlining findings
- Identification of security vulnerabilities
- Practical recommendations for continued improvement

Let's make sure the right plans, training, and protocols are in place to protect your house of worship, congregation, staff, and visitors.

---

# Professional Security for Every Service

Corsair Tactical Solutions provides onsite armed and unarmed security for:

- Weekly worship services
- Weekday programs
- Special events
- Funerals
- Revivals
- Church conferences
- Community outreach events
- Other church gatherings

Our Level III and Level IV licensed security officers—including veterans, former law enforcement personnel, and experienced security professionals—are trained to operate within the unique environment of a house of worship.

Our approach emphasizes:

- Prevention
- Professional visibility
- De-escalation
- Emergency response
- Respectful interaction with congregants and visitors

---

## Ongoing Church Security Partnership

We also assist churches with developing and training internal safety teams while providing ongoing instruction, assessments, and program reviews to ensure security plans remain effective, practical, and current.

From consultation and training to professional onsite protection, let's ensure your church has the right people, procedures, and plans in place.
