37. Blog Redesign – Transform "Blog" Into Corsair Tactical Insights

Priority: CRITICAL

Location:
https://www.corsairtacticalsolution.com/blog

The current blog implementation works from a technical standpoint, but it does not reflect the quality, authority, or professionalism of the Corsair Tactical Solutions brand.

Before any additional enhancements are completed, the Blog section must be redesigned into a true educational resource center.

The goal is to position Corsair Tactical Solutions as a trusted authority—not another course catalog.

A. Rename the Blog Experience

Current:
Blog

Update To:
Corsair Tactical Insights

Subtitle:

Professional insights, security guidance, training information, and practical advice from experienced security and firearms professionals.

Requirements:

* Update the Blog landing page heading.
* Preserve existing navigation functionality.
* Keep the URL as /blog.
* Maintain the site's existing design language.

B. Create a True Blog Homepage Experience

The current page feels too similar to the Courses page.

Redesign the layout to resemble a professional publication.

Requirements:

Display articles as editorial content cards featuring:

* Large featured image
* Article title
* Article category
* Publication date
* Estimated reading time
* Short excerpt (2–3 sentences)
* "Read Article" button

The page should immediately communicate:

"This is a resource center."

NOT:

"This is another sales page."

C. Add Featured Images to Every Article

Current Issue:

Some articles are missing imagery.

Every article must have a featured image.

Articles:

1. How Texas Churches Can Improve Their Security Posture

Suggested Category:
Church Safety

Suggested Imagery:
Church security team, congregation safety, planning session.

2. What Texas Businesses Should Know About Workplace Safety

Suggested Category:
Business Safety

Suggested Imagery:
Corporate environment, workplace preparedness, security planning.

3. Understanding Texas Security Officer Licensing Levels II–IV

Suggested Category:
Security Training

Suggested Imagery:
Professional security officers in training.

4. Texas License to Carry: What New Students Need to Know

Suggested Category:
LTC Education

Suggested Imagery:
Professional firearms instruction environment.

Requirements:

* Maintain consistent image ratios.
* Use authentic Corsair photography whenever available.
* Optimize images for fast loading.
* Ensure mobile responsiveness.
* Avoid stock-photo overuse whenever possible.

D. Improve Article Cards

Each article card should display:

* Featured image
* Category badge
* Publication date
* Reading time
* Article title
* Excerpt
* Read Article CTA

Remove:

* Any course-style formatting
* Registration language
* Sales-oriented styling
* Course pricing elements
* Buttons resembling checkout flows

Goal:

Readers should instantly recognize these as articles.

E. Improve Individual Articles

Update each article template.

Add:

Article Hero Section:

* Featured image
* Category label
* Article title
* Publication date
* Reading time
* Author attribution

Author:

By Corsair Tactical Solutions

Content Improvements:

* Clear heading hierarchy.
* Comfortable spacing.
* Improved readability.
* Mobile-friendly typography.
* Pull quotes or highlighted callouts if appropriate.

F. Related Resources Section

At the bottom of each article:

Display:

"You May Also Find Helpful"

Show 3 related resources.

Examples:

Church Security Article

Related Resources:

* Church Safety Services
* The 4D Protection Model™
* Workplace Safety Article

Security Licensing Article

Related Resources:

* Level II Training
* Level III Training
* Level IV Training

LTC Article

Related Resources:

* Texas LTC Certification / Basic Handgun
* Texas LTC Shooting Proficiency
* Defensive Shooting Skills

Goal:

Educate first.

Convert second.

Project Completion Standard

Corsair Tactical Insights should establish Corsair Tactical Solutions as an educational authority in security, training, and protection.
