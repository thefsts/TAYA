# Introduction

## New Introduction

The **4D Protection Model™** is Corsair Tactical Solutions' proprietary security framework designed to proactively reduce risk through progressive response strategies rather than reactive force.

Unlike traditional security models that focus primarily on physical intervention, the 4D Protection Model™ emphasizes prevention, awareness, communication, and professional decision-making before force is ever considered.

Every Corsair Tactical Solutions officer is trained to move through four sequential phases of protection, ensuring each situation is addressed at the lowest appropriate level of response.

---

# The Four Phases

## 1. Deter

**The First Line of Defense**

Visible security measures discourage threats before they become incidents.

This phase includes:

* Professional uniformed security presence
* Vehicle patrols
* Security signage
* Lighting assessments
* Access control
* Controlled entry points
* Professional visibility throughout the property

The objective is simple:

**Prevent criminal activity before it begins.**

---

## 2. Detect

**Early Identification Creates Better Outcomes**

Once deterrence is in place, trained professionals continuously monitor for suspicious activity.

Detection includes:

* Professional officer observation
* Surveillance systems
* Situational awareness
* Behavioral recognition
* Patrol reporting
* Incident documentation

Early detection creates valuable time and space to evaluate developing situations before they escalate.

---

## 3. Deflect

**The Corsair Tactical Solutions Difference**

This is the defining feature of the **4D Protection Model™**.

Rather than moving directly from detection to physical intervention, Corsair Tactical Solutions emphasizes communication, professionalism, and conflict resolution.

Officers are trained in:

* Verbal de-escalation
* Conflict management
* Professional communication
* Trauma-informed response techniques
* Crisis intervention principles
* Maintaining calm under pressure

The objective is to redirect potentially dangerous situations without unnecessary force whenever possible.

This phase reflects the Corsair Tactical Solutions philosophy:

**A well-trained officer is a communicator first and a physical responder only when absolutely necessary.**

---

## 4. Defend

**Professional Response When Necessary**

Physical intervention represents the final phase of the model and is only utilized when all previous layers have been exhausted or when an immediate threat to life or safety exists.

This phase may include:

* Protective intervention
* Emergency response
* Defensive tactics
* Incident containment
* Coordination with law enforcement
* Life safety response

Every action is guided by professional judgment, applicable laws, company policy, and the safety of clients, officers, and the public.

# Progressive Escalation Philosophy

## Progressive Escalation

The **4D Protection Model™** follows a structured escalation process.

Each phase activates only when the previous layer is insufficient to safely resolve the situation.

The model is intentionally designed to resolve incidents at the earliest possible stage, reducing unnecessary confrontation while improving safety for everyone involved.

By emphasizing prevention, awareness, communication, and only then physical response, Corsair Tactical Solutions delivers a higher standard of professional security services.
