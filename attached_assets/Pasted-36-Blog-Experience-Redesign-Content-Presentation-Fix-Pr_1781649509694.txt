36. Blog Experience Redesign & Content Presentation Fix

Priority: CRITICAL

Location:
https://www.corsairtacticalsolution.com/blog

The current blog implementation functions technically, but the user experience resembles a course sales page rather than a professional educational resource.

Before proceeding with any additional enhancements, please redesign the Blog section to present as a true article and knowledge center.

Do NOT redesign the entire website. Update only the Blog experience.

A. Replace Missing Blog Images

Current Issue:

Some blog articles are missing featured images.

Requirements:

* Every article must have a featured image.
* Images should be relevant to the article topic.
* Use authentic Corsair imagery whenever possible.
* If no Corsair image exists, use professional, industry-appropriate photography.

Current Articles Requiring Featured Images:

1. How Texas Churches Can Improve Their Security Posture
2. What Texas Businesses Should Know About Workplace Safety

* Featured images should have consistent dimensions.

* Optimize all images for performance.
* Verify desktop, tablet, and mobile presentation.
