# 4D Protection Model™ Content

Retain the complete content already approved:

### Deter

The first line of defense. Visible security presence, signage, lighting, and access control measures that discourage threats before they materialize.

### Detect

Surveillance systems, trained officer observation, and situational awareness protocols that identify potential threats early, creating time and space to respond.

### Deflect

The defining CTS differentiator, emphasizing verbal de-escalation, conflict management, and trauma-informed response techniques to resolve situations without unnecessary force whenever possible.

### Defend

The final escalation tier, activated only when the previous phases have been exhausted or bypassed, including physical intervention, emergency response, and coordination with law enforcement.

### Progressive Escalation

Explain that the model functions as a progressive escalation ladder, where each phase is activated only when the previous one is insufficient. The objective is always to resolve situations at the earliest possible stage, minimizing risk to personnel, clients, and bystanders.

Conclude with the Corsair Tactical Solutions philosophy:

> **A well-trained Security Officer is a communicator first and a physical responder only when necessary.**
