31. Final Visual & Content Audit – Please Verify These Additional Items

Priority: HIGH

Before closing this project, please perform one final visual and content audit to catch any remaining inconsistencies introduced during the updates.

A. Reusable Component Audit

Several sections of the website reuse the same components. Please verify that updates applied in one location were propagated everywhere those components appear.

Check for:

* Steve Hopwood bio sections
* Instructor profile cards
* Course cards
* Hero banners
* Testimonial sections
* CTA sections
* Featured course sections
* Event cards

Ensure all instances are displaying the latest content and imagery.

B. Image Cache Verification

Because multiple images have been replaced during this project:

* Verify the newest image files are being served.
* Confirm old optimized images are not being cached.
* Test in an incognito/private browser session.
* Test after clearing cache.
* Confirm Vercel image optimization is serving the updated assets.

C. Mobile Visual Review

Perform a full review on mobile devices and verify:

* Images are not clipped.
* Faces are not cut off.
* Text overlays remain readable.
* Buttons remain visible and clickable.
* Hero sections maintain proper proportions.
* Instructor portraits display correctly.
* Course cards remain visually consistent.

D. Tablet Review

Verify tablet layouts for:

* Portrait orientation
* Landscape orientation

Confirm:

* Proper spacing.
* Balanced layouts.
* Correct image scaling.
* No unexpected wrapping of text.

E. Desktop Review

Review the entire website on desktop and confirm:

* Images are sharp and centered.
* No layout shifts occur.
* Hover states continue functioning.
* Animations remain smooth.
* Section spacing remains consistent.

F. Typography & Content Consistency

Please verify:

* "Navy Veteran · 14 Yrs" appears consistently everywhere.
* No references remain to "12 Yrs."
* Course titles are identical across listings, detail pages, emails, and confirmations.
* Pricing is consistent throughout the site.
* Duration values match the approved course durations.
* Contact information is consistent.

G. Broken Link Audit

Perform a complete click-through of the production site and confirm:

* No 404 errors.
* No links redirect unintentionally to the homepage.
* Removed course redirects function properly.
* Navigation links work.
* Footer links work.
* Social links work.
* Phone and email links work.

H. SEO & Metadata Review

Verify that recent changes did not negatively impact SEO.

Check:

* Removed duplicate course redirects are functioning.
* No duplicate pages remain indexed.
* Updated course images have alt text.
* Updated instructor images have alt text.
* Metadata reflects current content.
* Structured data/schema is still valid.
