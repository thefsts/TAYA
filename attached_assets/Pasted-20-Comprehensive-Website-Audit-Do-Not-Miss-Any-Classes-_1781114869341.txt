Square Payment Requirements

Every paid offering on the website must have a functioning Square payment process.

Do not assume only the event page requires payment integration.

For EVERY paid class:

* Create or connect the appropriate Square item/payment link.
* Verify pricing matches the website.
* Verify checkout completes successfully.
* Verify payment confirmation is received.
* Verify the workflow works on desktop and mobile.

No class should require customers to call, email, or arrange payment separately unless specifically instructed.
