# PROBLEM

The recent implementation incorrectly moved AR-15 and Shotgun ammunition and rentals into `requiredFees`.

Only the range fee is required for these two courses.

Customers may bring their own firearm and ammunition, so rentals and ammunition purchases must remain optional selections.

- Base Course Price: $90.00
- Required Range Fee: $25.00

- Optional AR-15 Rental: $35.00

