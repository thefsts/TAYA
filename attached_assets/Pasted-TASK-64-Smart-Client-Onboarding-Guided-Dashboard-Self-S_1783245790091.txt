TASK 64 — Smart Client Onboarding, Guided Dashboard & Self-Service Training

PRIORITY: CRITICAL

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The FSTS Client Dashboard should require little to no training for new clients.

The dashboard should guide users through managing their website with built-in onboarding, contextual help, and step-by-step workflows.

The goal is for clients to feel confident using the platform without needing Full Stack Tech Solutions to teach them every feature.

DO NOT redesign the dashboard.

DO NOT change the established UI.

Build upon the existing dashboard with guided onboarding and contextual assistance.

==================================================
1. FIRST LOGIN EXPERIENCE
==================================================

When a client logs in for the first time:

Display a welcome experience.

Example:

Welcome to your Website Management Portal!

"We'll help you learn your dashboard in just a few minutes."

Provide:

• Start Guided Tour

• Skip for Now

• Watch Quick Overview

Progress should be saved automatically.

==================================================

The FSTS Client Dashboard should feel like a premium website management platform with built-in training.
