45. Cancellation Policy Update – No Refunds / Automatic Class Transfer

Priority: HIGH

Please update the cancellation policy throughout the entire website to reflect the company's current enrollment policy.

This policy should be consistent anywhere cancellations, refunds, registrations, or payment terms are discussed.

## New Cancellation Policy

Replace any existing refund or cancellation language with the following policy:

### Cancellation & Class Transfer Policy

Students who are unable to attend their scheduled class must notify Corsair Tactical Solutions as soon as possible.

**Course registrations are non-refundable.** If a student is unable to attend their scheduled class, their registration fee will **not** be refunded. Instead, the student will be **automatically transferred to the next available class** of the same course at no additional charge.

This policy helps ensure students retain the full value of their registration while allowing class schedules to remain organized.
