Task 57 — Final Professionalization & Human Quality Review

Priority: CRITICAL

Project Goal

Perform a complete quality review of the entire Corsair Tactical Solutions website.

The objective is for the website to feel like a professionally designed, custom-developed security company website with years of refinement—not a generic template or automatically generated site.

Do NOT redesign the website.

Do NOT change the established branding or layout.

Instead, improve every detail that contributes to craftsmanship, polish, consistency, and professionalism.

==================================================

1. CONTENT REVIEW

==================================================

Review every page for:

• Repetitive wording
• Generic marketing language
• Duplicate phrases
• Inconsistent terminology
• Placeholder content
• Awkward sentence structure
• Mechanical writing

Rewrite where appropriate so the copy sounds like it came directly from Corsair Tactical Solutions.

Maintain a consistent voice throughout the site.

==================================================

2. BRAND CONSISTENCY

==================================================

Ensure every page reinforces the same brand identity.

Corsair Tactical Solutions should consistently be presented as:

• Professional Security Services Company
• Texas Licensed Security Contractor
• Executive Protection Provider
• Private Investigation Firm
• Security Officer Training Academy
• Firearms Training Provider

Security Services should always be presented before Training.

• Heading hierarchy
• Icon styles

• Image treatments

Nothing should appear inconsistent from page to page.
