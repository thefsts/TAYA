# FSTS Website Operating System - IP Asset Register

**Owner:** Full Stack Tech & Solutions LLC  
**Product family:** FSTS Website Operating System (FSTS-WOS) / Client Dashboard for Sites  
**Status:** Private proprietary commercial platform  
**Year:** 2026

## Strategic purpose

FSTS-WOS is both an internal production platform and a commercial software asset. FSTS uses the platform to accelerate website creation, onboarding, administration, maintenance, and client self-service, while retaining the ability to license the platform to outside businesses.

## Commercial licensing channels

1. **FSTS SaaS:** customers use the platform under FSTS branding.
2. **Enterprise:** larger organizations receive expanded users, organizations, integrations, controls, support and contractual service levels.
3. **White Label:** authorized customers operate a branded presentation of the hosted FSTS platform while FSTS retains underlying IP.
4. **Agency / Reseller:** authorized agencies or service providers may provision/manage customer accounts subject to reseller terms.
5. **OEM / Embedded:** may be offered under a separately negotiated agreement when another software product embeds FSTS technology.
6. **Source-code license:** not included in standard white-label rights; requires a separate expressly negotiated agreement if ever offered.

## White-label ownership controls

White-label agreements should expressly state that:

- FSTS owns the platform and underlying technology.
- The customer owns or controls its contributed brand assets/content/data as contractually defined.
- Branding the software does not constitute an assignment of software ownership.
- Improvements to FSTS reusable technology remain FSTS IP unless expressly assigned.
- Repository/source-code access is excluded unless separately granted.
- Reverse engineering, unauthorized cloning and creation of competing derivative platforms are restricted to the extent permitted by law.
- Termination removes the customer's right to continue operating the FSTS platform except for agreed export/wind-down rights.
