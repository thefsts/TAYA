## Strategic purpose

FSTS-WOS is both an internal production platform and a commercial software asset. FSTS uses the platform to accelerate website creation, onboarding, administration, maintenance, and client self-service, while retaining the ability to license the platform to outside businesses.

## Commercial licensing channels

1. **FSTS SaaS** - customers use the platform under FSTS branding.
2. **Enterprise** - expanded organizations, integrations, controls, support and service levels.
3. **White Label** - authorized branded presentation of the hosted FSTS platform while FSTS retains underlying IP.
4. **Agency / Reseller** - authorized agencies/service providers provision or manage downstream accounts subject to reseller terms.
5. **OEM / Embedded** - separately negotiated embedding of FSTS technology.
6. **Source-code license** - excluded from standard white-label rights and separately negotiated if ever offered.

## IP classification standard

FSTS Core IP and Product-Specific IP remain FSTS property. Client-specific branding/content/configuration/data is governed by customer contracts. Third-Party and Partner IP retain their respective rights. Joint/Contract IP exists only where an executed agreement expressly establishes it.

## White-label ownership controls

White-label agreements should preserve FSTS ownership of the platform and improvements, customer rights in contributed marks/content/data, exclude source/repository access unless separately granted, restrict unauthorized cloning/reverse engineering to the extent permitted by law, and provide termination/export/debranding rules.
