# Contributor & Chain-of-Title Policy

**Product:** FSTS Website Operating System / Client Dashboard  
**Owner:** Full Stack Tech & Solutions LLC  
**Founder/CEO:** Curtis Dorelle Weems  
**Status:** Internal governance policy - attorney review recommended

Material proprietary contributions by employees, contractors, agencies, consultants, collaborators and other contributors should be governed by written confidentiality and IP ownership terms. Where legally appropriate, use work-made-for-hire language, present assignment to FSTS and further-assurances obligations.

AI-assisted development must be reviewed for originality, security, third-party licensing and confidential-information exposure. Client credentials, private client content/data, production secrets and unauthorized third-party source code must not be submitted to AI systems.

Client websites, logos, copy, photos, forms, course/event materials and other client-provided content remain separately classified from FSTS WOS Core IP. Client configuration or payment for service does not transfer the dashboard, CMS, forms builder, media systems, AI tools, preview systems, health systems, APIs, schemas or reusable platform modules.

Custom client development should identify whether the result is Client Content, a licensed custom deliverable, FSTS Background IP, or an FSTS reusable platform enhancement. No joint ownership should arise by implication.

Maintain contributor identity/relationship, agreements, contribution period, material contribution description, repository/release evidence, introduced third-party materials and offboarding/access-removal records as applicable.

This policy does not replace signed agreements.
